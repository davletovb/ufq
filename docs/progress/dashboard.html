Ground truths

constraints, not opinions

The context the design must absorb. Changing one requires a Decisions Log entry; the chips are the requirements that descend from it.

GT-1

The deciding unit is the household, not the child

Career choice is a family portfolio decision; social evaluation is a real utility term. A tool that only talks to the teenager gets overridden at the dinner table.

The core deliverable is a negotiation artifact for the family (M3); the parent is a first-class user (R-2).

M3R-2P-3
GT-2

Occupational awareness is narrow, and Soviet-inflected at the parent level

An interest inventory administered over unfamiliar occupations measures familiarity and prestige, not interest.

Exposure (M1) precedes and feeds assessment (M2); exposure history is a first-class data stream.

M1M2-F2P-1
GT-3

Self-report psychometrics degrade in predictable ways

Acquiescence bias, social desirability, unfamiliar Likert conventions, the instinct that every test has a correct answer, and a parent beside the child on a shared phone.

Format-level countermeasures, not translation-level ones. No 'test' language, no visible score.

M2-F1P-2E-7
GT-4

The prestige economy and the wage economy have diverged, and lock-in comes early

Medicine and law are oversubscribed relative to returns; subject-block choice around grades 9-10 forecloses paths before discovery normally happens.

Primary audience grades 7-9; every recommendation carries a financing path and a subject-block plan; vocational routes get production parity.

M4E-10R-1
GT-5

Constraints are structured by gender and geography

Some families cap a daughter's options by marriage timing; some professions are socially coded; mobility to Tashkent is a live constraint for regional children.

Constraints enter only as explicit family-stated inputs, never as platform-encoded rules. The possibility space is expanded through content.

M2-F6E-5M1-F5
GT-6

Infrastructure realities

Telegram is the distribution layer; cheap shared Android devices; thin regional bandwidth; two Uzbek scripts plus Russian; in-country hosting required; users are minors.

Mini App primary with full web parity; single-source content with transliteration; low-bandwidth defaults; residency, consent and shared-device safety are architectural.

NFR-1NFR-2E-6E-8
GT-7

The state has already created our category

The September 2025 decree on mandatory school advisors turned a vague obligation into a named, staffed, under-tooled role.

The B2B pitch is the advisor's operating system, not extra work. Private schools are the beachhead.
